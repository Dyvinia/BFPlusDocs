Star Cards

Each of the four base classes boasts an array of new Star Cards, creating new ways to play as the Troopers. Instill your enemies with dread with Dead Man's Switch, torch the halls with the heavy Flamethrower, or support your allies with the Medical Droid and Gonk Droid. Tailor each of the new heroes featured in Battlefront Plus to your liking, with each of them packing their own set of custom Star Cards to allow players to enhance their playstyles with improved stats and ability combos.

Hint: Hover over images to reveal Star Card descriptions

Troopers

All Classes

Tough Fighter

Defeating enemies will briefly grant damage resistance.

Radio

Deploys a radio to play some nice tunes.

Assault

Dead Man's Switch

Drop a live Thermal Detonator when killed.

Smart Mortar

Deploys an automated mortar which detects enemies at medium range to fire explosive munitions at.

Thermal Imploder

The THERMAL IMPLODER's compressed heat wave creates a massive implosion, dealing damage in a large radius.

Instigator

The first blaster shot on an enemy deals extra damage. This effect can be applied again to the same enemy after a short duration.

Heavy

Withstanding

Gain bonus health when your MIDDLE ABILITY is activated.

Gonk Droid

Deploys a GNK-series power droid to grant bonus recharge speed and to nearby allies.

Rocket Launcher

The ROCKET LAUNCHER fires an explosive projectile.

Flamethrower

Burn out the enemy with the BT X-42 Heavy Flame Projector. While deployed, you passively regenerate health and take reduced damage.

Officer

Mechanical Companion

While your RIGHT ABILITY is active, you will slowly regenerate health.

Medical Droid

Deploys an FX-7 medical assistant to periodically spray bacta mist, healing nearby allies.

Stim Shot

Fires a projectile to provide healing and bonus health to allies within a small radius of impact. Holding the cooling button will use the Stim Shot to boost its user.

Stun Grenade

Grenade that stuns enemies, freezing them in place for a short duration.

Specialist

Quickdraw

Upon successful shots with your primary weapon, your secondary blaster will become momentarily empowered, causing it to deal more damage to enemies.

Ion Neutralizer

Finely tuned variant of the T-7 Disruptor Rifle that is capable of delivering heavy ion damage to vehicles at long range, but is ineffective against infantry.

Smoke Grenade

Grenade that deploys a cloud of smoke, obscuring vision in its area.

Time Bomb

Deploy a highly explosive device set to detonate after a short time.

Reinforcements

Sentinel

Sentinel Defense Master

The Sentinel's defensive ability is stronger.

Evasion

You have +1 Evade and faster Evade recharge times.

Guardian

Regain health when defeating an enemy while you are near allies. The amount of health regained increases, to a maximum of 100, with the number of allies present.

Reckless Protection

For every ability in recharge, you momentarily gain increased resistance to incoming damage.

Sustained Aggression

Performing 5 successful melee attacks without taking damage will temporarily increase your melee damage.

Heroes

Ahsoka

Jar'Kai Mastery

Ahsoka will deal extra damage with SABER FLURRY if it is performed immediately after a successful SABER THROW attack.

Great Stride

The active time of MORAL SPIRIT is extended.

Fearless

SABER FLURRY has increased range, enabling Ahsoka to lunge toward more distant enemies, but the recharge time of the ability is slower.

I'm No Jedi

Ahsoka's basic lightsaber attacks, after being used to defeat an enemy, will deal bonus damage for a brief period. Her maximum health is also decreased.

Skywalker's Student

The recharge time of SABER THROW is reduced.

Cal Kestis

Persistence

Cal deals bonus damage to enemies affected by Force Stasis.

Reforged

SINGLE-BLADED lightsaber attacks deal more damage if they immediately follow successful attacks with the DOUBLE-BLADED lightsaber.

Stim Potency

STIM CANISTER restores more health.

Peacekeeper

Enemies are affected by FORCE STASIS for a longer duration, but the ability has a slower cooldown.

Path Forward

Attacking enemies with the DOUBLE-BLADED lightsaber stance will reduce ability recharge times even more.

Captain Rex

Blaster Inspection

Every time he kills an enemy, Captain Rex and friendly troopers around him gain a permanent 3% decrease to their blaster overheat per bolt to a maximum.

Still Holds Up

The damage resistance gained from GENERATION ONE lasts longer.

Outranking Experience

Increases the radius of UNCONVENTIONAL TACTICS.

I'm No Jedi

Captain Rex's Dual DC-17 pistols deal extra damage while he is above 90% health.

Fry Their Circuits

DROID POPPERS inflict lingering damage for a short period, but have a longer recharge time.

Commander Cody

Blast Them

Attacking a target marked by Attack Order will additionally grant resistance to incoming damage.

You'll Be Needing This

The cooldown of ENERGY WALL is reduced.

Always a Way

Defeating enemies with the BATTLE RIFLE will replenish its active time.

You Can Count On Us

If he is within 18 meters of three friendly units, Commander Cody and his close allies will regenerate some health.

Armored Up

Commander Cody gains some bonus health whenever he defeats an enemy. This effect can be stacked limitlessly, but drains slowly over time.

Din Djarin

Extension Cord

Din Djarin can target enemies further away with GRAPPLE STRIKE.

This Is The Way

While the AMBAN PHASE-PULSE BLASTER is equipped, replenish its active time by defeating enemies with WHISTLING BIRDS or the DARKSABER.

Phalanx

Din Djarin takes less damage while using WHISTLING BIRDS.

Warm or Cold

When Din Djarin defeats an enemy with a headshot, his abilities receive a short boost to recharge speed.

One Defeats Twenty

Din Djarin inflicts more damage when striking an enemy with the DARKSABER.

Hunter

Predator

In addition to his VIBRO-KNIFE dealing more damage, Hunter will disappear from enemy radar whenever he defeats an enemy in melee combat.

Nothing but Trouble

While evading, Hunter has increased damage resistance.

Rush Them Head On

COMBAT PROWESS grants additional bonus health and lasts slightly longer.

Wasn't Gonna Hurt Ya

Hunter's STUN MODE builds less heat when fired.

We Do What We Do

Hunter's health regeneration is faster and starts sooner.

Maz Kanata

An Acquired Taste

Damage can be reflected back on Maz Kanata's attackers from CHANGE IN FORTUNE more frequently.

We Must Face Them

The fire rate of the DH-17R is faster than normal while Maz Kanata is above 90% health, but slower when she falls below that percentage.

Fuel Reserves

The cooldown time of BURN DASH is reduced.

Air Dispute

Damaging enemies will briefly prevent Maz's jetpack from draining fuel.

Live Long Enough

Maz Kanata takes reduced damage while CHANGE IN FORTUNE is active, but the ability's active time is shorter.

Merrin

From the Shadows

Merrin has one extra TELEPORTATION, but each use decrements her Power Meter.

Survivors, We Adapt

While Merrin's Power Meter is at max level, her spear does significantly increased damage.

Strike Now

The initial targeting range of MAGICK ROOTS is increased.

Might of Dathomir

Merrin's FIREBALL has improved recharge time and staggers enemies on direct impact.

Lend Me Strength

Merrin's health regeneration is faster and starts sooner.

Nien Nunb

Bomb Arming Speed

The PROXIMITY BOMB will arm and activate faster when deployed, but its recharge time is increased.

Brave Sullustan

Nien Nunb's maximum health is increased.

Sullustan Sharpshooter

When Nien Nunb defeats an enemy with a headshot using his RAPID PULSE CANNON, his abilities are taken out of recharge and granted a cooldown bonus.

Expert Weapons Handling

The cooling success states on Nien Nunb's DH-17 are longer.

Buddy Turret

Nien Nunb will receive damage reduction when attacking enemies with his DH-17 while the AUGMENTED TURRET is deployed, but the recharge time of the ability is twice as long.

Padmé Amidala

Power Efficiency

R2-D2 has a shorter recharge time.

Inspirational

Increases the radius of ROYAL RESOLVE.

Unwavering

Padmé has increased maximum health regeneration.

Aggressive Negotiations

When defeating enemies with OVERCHARGE, Padmé temporarily takes reduced damage based on her blaster's power level.

A Path to Follow

The duration of ROYAL RESOLVE is increased, but it has a longer recharge time.

Shriv Suurgav

Wartender

BACTA COCKTAIL provides additional healing, but its recharge time is increased.

Got a Bad Idea

After Shriv defeats 4 enemies with the REPULSOR CANNON, the recharge rate of all of his abilities is increased.

Taking Risks

For every enemy Shriv defeats with the E-5C, its heat will be reduced.

Personal Space

Shriv's REPULSOR CANNON fires additional projectiles.

Danger Leader

If Shriv is within 10 meters of two friendly units, his E-5C will fire faster and his nearby allies will begin regenerating a small amount of health.

Villains

Asajj Ventress

Sister of the Night

The duration of VANISH is increased, allowing Ventress to remain invisible even longer, but the recharge time is slower.

Sith Assassin

Performing a VANISH STRIKE will inflict even more damage and reveal the positions of nearby enemies upon defeating an opponent.

Talzin's Protection

Asajj Ventress has increased maximum health regeneration.

Suspenseful Grip

FORCE GRASP has one extra use.

Extra Starblades

Ventress throws three projectiles at once when using her STARBLADES, but the ability can only be used once before needing to recharge.

Captain Cardinal

Color of Power

All of Captain Cardinal's abilities have a slightly extended duration.

Still Standing

Captain Cardinal takes reduced damage while STEADFAST is active.

Finalized

Captain Cardinal deals more melee damage while FINALIZER is active.

Ensuring Progress

Lessens the vulnerability penalty from ABSOLUTION.

Effecting Change

Whenever Captain Cardinal defeats an enemy with the F-11D, his abilities recharge faster for a very brief moment.

Commander Pyre

All Your Efforts

Defeating enemies with THUNDERER LEGION will restore its active time.

Commanding Shine

The bonus health awarded by GOLD ZONE is increased.

Maintaining Order

The recharge time of SEIZE THIS MOMENT is reduced.

Complementary

When Commander Pyre defeats an enemy, he will gain a small amount of damage resistance for a short duration. Maintaining quick killstreaks will stack this value.

None of Your Concern

Commander Pyre's health regeneration is faster and starts sooner.

Dagan Gera

Can't Hide From Me

The radius of ILLUSION will not decay for a short delay.

Forsaken

For every enemy damaged by FORCE ORBS, Dagan replenishes health.

Beckoning

FORCE BOMB will be more powerful if it is used while ILLUSION is active.

Meager

Dagan Gera has increased maximum health, but the delay before health regeneration is also increased.

Sever the Past

For a brief duration after any of his abilities end, Dagan Gera will deal extra damage with his lightsaber attacks, but will also take more damage himself.

Dengar

More Toys

The cooldown on Explosive Array is reduced and the bombs have a longer fuse time.

Job Ready

Dengar has increased maximum health.

Take a Dirt Nap

BASHING SKULLS deals more damage, but has increased recharge time.

Spraying Bolts

Extends the duration of FRENZIED BLAST.

You're Next

The damage resistance Dengar gains from defeating an enemy is increased.

Gideon Hask

Lingering Embers

Enemies damaged by the flames of INFERNO BLAST will be afflicted with lingering damage for several seconds.

Imperial Valor

While Hask is using BLAST CANNON, his nearby allies will receive damage reduction, which increases in strength with every 3 enemies defeated to a maximum.

Crimson Star

The duration of ROUTING TRAITORS is extended, as is its range.

Special Forces

If he is within 15 meters of two friendly units, Hask and his close allies will take reduced damage.

Returned Favor

Hask regains slightly more health when defeating an enemy, but his ability cooldowns are slightly longer.

Grand Admiral Thrawn

Piece by Piece

The minimum time between attacks to charge up KNOW THE ENEMY is reduced.

Strategic Mastery

The radius of DIRECT COMMAND increases while the ability is active.

Test Their Mettle

CHIMAERA STRIKE recharges faster.

Momentary Setback

Thrawn's health regeneration is faster and begins sooner.

Ascendant

Thrawn captures objectives twice as fast and has increased maximum health.

Greedo

Who Shot First

The fire rate of the DT-12 is faster than normal while Greedo is above 90% health, but slower when he falls below that percentage.

Koon T'chuta

The THERMAL DETONATOR from Greedo's BRASH BLAST deals increased damage.

Lucky I Found You

Enemies revealed by Greedo's HIGH SENSES will restore extra health to him upon being defeated.

Measured Confidence

Greedo's CONFIDENCE BOOST has a shorter duration and recharge time.

Ma Klounkee

Greedo's DT-12 builds less heat when firing.

Jango Fett

No Mistakes This Time

Eliminating an enemy affected by SABERDART will grant Jango an extensive recharge bonus.

Always a Pleasure

Whenever enemy Heroes are nearby, Jango Fett receives damage reduction. This effect is amplified if Jedi are present.

Discretion is My Specialty

Damaging enemies with the WRIST FLAMETHROWER will restore a portion of Jango Fett's health, but its recharge time is doubled.

Evasion

Jango Fett has +1 Evade, but slower Evade recharge time.

Just a Simple Man

ADAPTIVE ARMOR has an increased detection radius.

Second Sister

Like All the Others

The Second Sister replenishes even more health from FORCE DRAIN.

Enduring the Pain

Trilla's torturous transformation into the Second Sister has taught her to embrace the pain. She will briefly gain increased damage resistance upon taking any hits.

Perfected Technique

SINGLE-BLADED lightsaber attacks deal more damage if they immediately follow successful attacks with the DOUBLE-BLADED lightsaber.

Let's Finish This

After one enemy has been struck by CUNNING SLASH, the attack will inflict weakness on follow-up hits.

I Expected More

The Second Sister's maximum health is increased.
