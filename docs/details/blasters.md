Whatever your choose, Battlefront Plus offers the opportunity to tailor Trooper loadouts to your liking.

## Attachments
<p id="berserker"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Berserker.svg"> Berserker:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases damage while hip-firing, but decreases damage while aiming.</span></p>
<p id="burstmode"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Trigger.svg"> Burst Mode:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Enables a burst mode with each pull of the trigger.</span></p>
<p id="chargehack"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Trigger.svg"> Charge Hack:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Alters weapon charge-up at the cost of lower potential.</span></p>
<p id="dualzoom"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Scope.svg"> Dual Zoom:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Macroscope that can toggle between two magnification levels while zooming.</span></p>
<p id="eliteblaster"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Elite.svg"> Elite Blaster:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases damage and reconfigures the weapon to fire in bursts, with distinct purple blaster bolts.</span></p>
<p id="escapeartist"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_EscapeArtist.svg"> Escape Artist:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases movement speed, but reduces zoom magnification.</span></p>
<p id="explosive"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_BarrelExtension.svg"> Explosive Shot:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Unstable plasma that explodes on impact, dispersing the bolt damage across a small area. Due to its volatile nature, the blaster builds heat significantly faster.</span></p>
<p id="flashlight"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Barrel.svg"> Flashlight:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Barrel mounted light source that reveals enemies while zooming.</span></p>
<p id="focusedfire"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Stock.svg"> Focused Fire:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Improves recoil at the cost of worse sprint time recovery.</span></p>
<p id="gunslinger"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Grip.svg"> Gunslinger:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Improves sprint time recovery, but increases recoil.</span></p>
<p id="heavyhanded"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_CoolingCell.svg"> Heavy Handed:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Improves heat drop delay and rate, but reduces movement speed.</span></p>
<p id="ionshot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Barrel.svg"> Ion Shot:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Ionized bolts that increase damage against turrets, vehicles, and shields, but reduced damage against infantry, special units, and heroes.</span></p>
<p id="lightshot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_CoolingCell.svg"> Light Shot:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Improves cooling at the cost of damage.</span></p>
<p id="multishot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_SecondaryFire.svg"> Multi-Shot Configuration:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">While firing from the hip, the blaster is configured to fire multiple projectiles at once with reduced damage. Reduces zoom magnification.</span></p>
<p id="nightvision"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Scope.svg"> Night Vision:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Digital scope capable of revealing enemy troops even in the darkest of environments.</span></p>
<p id="precisionblaster"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Elite.svg"> Precision Blaster:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases range and projectile speed, with distinct purple trails, but disables active cooldown.</span></p>
<p id="altfire"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_SecondaryFire.svg"> Secondary Fire:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Enable a secondary fire that charges up a powerful shot while aiming.</span></p>
<p id="scope"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Scope.svg"> Improved Zoom:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Scope with increased magnification.</span></p>
<p id="seeker"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Stock.svg"> Seeker Tactics:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases movement speed while aiming, but reduces blaster bolt speed.</span></p>
<p id="sharpshooter"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Sharpshooter.svg"> Sharpshooter:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases damage while aiming, but decreases damage while hip-firing.</span></p>
<p id="phasmashot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Barrel.svg"> Stabilizer:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">The blaster has terrible accuracy when first fired, but quickly settles into a tight firing pattern.</span></p>

### Unique Attachments

<p id="ca87-crouchcontrol"><strong><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_RangeFinder.svg"> Crouch Control:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Modifies the CA-87 to condense all its blaster bolts while crouched, but decreases its rate of fire.</span></p>
<p id="dc15s-powercell"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_HeavyHanded.svg"> Power Cell:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Reduces the blaster's cooling speed to upgrade its damage.</span></p>
<p id="dp23-choke"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_BarrelExtension.svg"> Choke:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Tightens the shock blaster's spread, but worsens recoil.</span></p>
<p id="dt29-cooling"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_CoolingCell.svg"> Cooling Cell:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Enables supercooling of the blaster at the cost of heat build-up.</span></p>
<p id="ee3-sugi"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Elite.svg"> Sugi:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Outfits the EE-3 with similar settings used by the bounty hunter Sugi, making it semi-automatic with blue blaster bolts.</span></p>
<p id="firepuncher-longlife"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Barrel.svg"> Extended Life:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Increases the live time of the Firepuncher's projectile, but reduces its speed.</span></p>
<p id="firepuncher-fuze"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_BarrelExtension.svg"> Fuze:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Creates an explosion when the Firepuncher projectile's life ends, but reduces its active time.</span></p>
<p id="hf94-vanthrefrigeration"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_CoolingCell.svg"> Second Chance:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Trades some of the HF-94's power and range for the ability to fire a second shot without overheating.</span></p>
<p id="sonic-fast"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_BarrelExtension.svg"> Accelerated Sphere:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Expends a portion of the sonic sphere's blast damage to increase its travel speed.</span></p>
<p id="t12-oneshot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Trigger.svg"> Both Barrels:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Fire from both barrels simultaneously.</span></p>
<p id="t12-slug"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_RangeFinder.svg"> Slug:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Fire a single slug for improved accuracy and range. Additionally enables the scope and increases zoom.</span></p>
<p id="t39p-invertrange"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_RangeFinder.svg"> Ranger Conversion:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Converted blaster pack inverts blaster damage drop-off and reduces rate of fire.</span></p>
<p id="v6d-coldwar"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_ColdWar.svg"> Pulse Barrage:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Charges up a rapid burst that fires all projectiles.</span></p>
<p id="valken-apex"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_TripleTake.svg"> Triple Take:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">Further modifies the Valken-38x to fire a third blaster bolt, but reduces its cooling power.</span></p>
<p id="valken-verticalshot"><strong><img class="ability" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/attachments/Attach_Scope.svg"> Reconfiguration Scope:</strong> <span style="opacity: 0.69;">While aiming down sights, the Valken-38x will reconfigure to fire its blaster bolts vertically parallel. Additionally, the scope magnification is increased.</span></p>
