### Install

#### Installing Mods in Kyber V2

1. Download **Kyber V2**.

2. Open the `MOD BROWSER`.

3. Select **Battlefront Plus** and click `INSTALL`.

4. Open `MOD COLLECTIONS` and apply **Battlefront Plus** to your `ACTIVE MOD LIST`.

5. Click `Launch`.

___

#### Using Kyber V2

##### To Join a Server:

- Click the `PLAY` tab to access the server browser.
- Select the server you wish to join and click `JOIN`.
    - If you have not already downloaded and installed **Battlefront Plus**, it will be handled automatically and you will be prompted to join once it is ready.

##### To Create & Host a Server:

- Click the `HOST` tab to access the server creation menu.
- Select `MOD COLLECTIONS` and select the profile you installed **Battlefront Plus** to.
- To customize your server:
    - Create your `SERVER NAME`.
        - You can also fill in `SERVER TAGS` to make your server easier to find.
        - You can also fill out a `DESCRIPTION` so people know what your server is about.
    - Open `MAP & GAMEMODE ROTATION` to select the maps and gamemodes you want to use in your map rotation.
    - Under the `ADVANCED` tab, you can setup:
        - A `PASSWORD` to lock your server.
        - The `MAX PLAYERS` to limit the amount of people who may join.
        - `PROXIMITY CHAT` to enable or disable in-world voice chat from players on your server.
- Click `START SERVER`.
