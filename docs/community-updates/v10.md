# Battlefront+ v10 Announcement

![v10cover](../assets/gallery/maincover_v2.webp){ .round-corners }

Hello there, welcome back to another Community Update for Battlefront Plus! We know it’s been a very long time since the last content drop (over a year), but we’ve been hard at work on our grandest update yet, with even greater things to come down the road. Without any further delay, let's dive right in to what you can expect from the upcoming dual-release of Kyber V2 and Battlefront+ V10.

____

<h3 id="kyber-v2"><strong><img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/kyber.svg"> KYBER V2, INSTANT ACTION, AND INSTALLATION</strong></h3> 

![kybercover](../assets/community-updates/v10/banner_kyberv2.webp){ .round-corners }

Our longtime partners at Armchair Developers have been hard at work on creating Kyber V2, a private server system for Battlefront II, now featuring a suite of new and improved features from the original release, such as dedicated 24/7 servers, built-in mod browser with hands-free installation, proximity voice chat, stats tracking, and more.

Frosty Mod Manager is no longer supported. Going forward, Battlefront Plus will be launched exclusively through Kyber V2's built-in mod browser for its quick and easy installation. Battlefront Plus will now also support Kyber private servers and Instant Action with a single version, allowing players to freely swap between online and offline play whenever they choose, just as it is in vanilla.

____

<h3 id="arcade"><strong><img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/mazkanata/MazKanata_Jetpack.svg"> ARCADE UPGRADES</strong></h3> 

![arcade](../assets/community-updates/v10/banner_deathtrooper_02.webp){ .round-corners }

Arcade has received substantial improvements to its gameplay and stability, creating more interesting ways to play fully offline and in split-screen coop. Autoplayers have replaced BattleAI, greatly increasing the capabilities of the bots and even allowing them to spawn in as Reinforcements and Heroes.

Onslaught has been overhauled into a survival-type mode. There is no victory, you simply must fight for as long as you can against an increasingly strong enemy force until they either overwhelm you or you give up.

Many of the base game maps have been given new daytime and weather variants. Bask in the beauty of glowing flora during a <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/map-variants/icons/Daytimes_Night.svg"> Felucian night , brace for irritation in <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/map-variants/icons/Daytimes_Weather.svg"> Jakku's sandstorm, battle under <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/map-variants/icons/Daytimes_Weather.svg"> Kashyyyk's cloud cover just like in *Revenge of the Sith*, and so much more. 

To that end, many of the game's existing daytime and weather variants that were absent from Supremacy have now been added, such as <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/map-variants/icons/Daytimes_Sunny.svg"> Naboo during the afternoon.

The Honor Guard and Shock Trooper are some of the oldest custom reinforcements ever made and are first up on our round of changes. Both are once again Enforcers, reacquiring all of the appropriate Star Cards. For the Honor Guard, the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/honorguard/HonorGuard_A280CFE.svg"> **A280-CFE** has been replaced with the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/honorguard/HonorGuard_TripMine.svg"> **CONCUSSION MINE**, a proximity-triggered explosive device that is light on damage, but will knock back and daze enemies. As for the Shock Trooper, per very popular request, the primary blaster has been swapped back to the **DLT-19**, with the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/shocktrooper/ShockTrooper_SE14C.svg"> **SE-14c** being moved to the left ability, replacing the<img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/shocktrooper/ShockTrooper_BactaGrenade.svg">**BACTA GRENADE**. As an added mechanic, enemies caught within the electric smoke of the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/shocktrooper/ShockTrooper_ShockLauncher.svg">**SHOCK LAUNCHER** are slowed down and will sustain severe damage if affected by any abilities which disrupt blasters.

Many of our Infiltrators have been reworked, starting with the Rebel Saboteur. She boasts a completely new look and a revamped her scanner ability. When activating the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelsaboteur/RebelSaboteur_Scanner.svg">**REBEL INTEL** ability, it will initiate a scan pulse every few seconds, starting at just two pulses in total. Each enemy defeated by her <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelsaboteur/RebelSaboteur_Baton.svg">**TRUNCHEON ATTACK** will increase the number of total scan pulses by one.

Next is the Smuggler, who's also sporting a new look alongside new and reworked abilities. Instead of the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/niktosmuggler/NiktoSmuggler_ShoulderCharge.svg">**SHOULDER CHARGE**, which we found to be redundant within the Resistance faction, the Smuggler can now throw a <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/niktosmuggler/NiktoSmuggler_Molotov.svg">**BOTTLE BOMB** to spew fire over a small area. The <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/niktosmuggler/NiktoSmuggler_Scanner.svg">**DATA THEFT** ability will initiate one scan pulse to reveal enemies for an extended duration. The radius starts off small for a scanner, requiring the elimination of revealed enemies to increase it.

As for the Aqua Droid, the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/aquadroid/AQ_Recon.svg">**RECON** ability has been replaced with <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/aquadroid/AQ_Head.svg">**TRIDENT PURSUENT**, which replenishes health and abilities by attacking a marked enemy. Defeating your target will significantly reduce the cooldown of your next use of the ability. An added mechanic for <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/aquadroid/AQ_Scanner.svg">**SONAR SCAN** will increase the reveal duration if the ability is used three times in a short time, synergizing well with <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/aquadroid/AQ_Head.svg">**TRIDENT PURSUENT**. Then, for the Aqua Droid's last ability, <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/aquadroid/AQ_Spear.svg">**SPEARHEAD** will increase your damage output towards anybody affected by either of the other two abilities.

Replacing the ARC Trooper's <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/arctrooper/ARC_ShieldRush.svg">**SHIELD RUSH** ability is <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/arctrooper/ARC_Barrage.svg">**BLASTER BARRAGE**, which unleashes a deadly hail of blaster fire from his dual pistols. For each enemy defeated with the ability, the duration of its next use will increase by one second.

The Purge Trooper Commander now features his own voicelines and, like with the other Infiltrators mentioned so far, has also received an updated scanner ability, as <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/purgetroopercommander/PurgeTrooperCommander_Scanner.svg">**INQUISITION** will now activate a singular pulse to reveal enemies in a large radius for an extended duration. That duration is doubled if any Jedi are detected.

![rework_saboteur](../assets/community-updates/v10/banner_saboteur_01.webp){ .round-corners } 

Last up for our Infiltrators is the Viper Probe Droid, which sees its <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/viper/Viper_SelfDestruct.svg">**SELF-DESTRUCT** revamped, where upon 6 seconds from activation or death while active, it will explode. Replacing the old <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/viper/Viper_Support_Old.svg">**MECHANICAL COMPANION** ability is a two-shot burst <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/viper/Viper_Support.svg">**SMOKE GRENADE** launcher to obscure the enemy's vision. Best of all, after being teased over 2 years ago, the Probe Droid will rotate its head independently from its body.

Now for the rest of our updated Enforcers. The Combat Engineer and Tactical Droid have both received new abilities inspired by the HAADs from the classic games, replacing the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/cloneengineer/CloneEngineer_CoolingGrenade.svg">**SUPERCOOLING GRENADE** and <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/tacticaldroid/TacticalDroid_BubbleShield.svg">**ENHANCED SQUAD SHIELD** respectively. Regarding the former, picking up the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/cloneengineer/CloneEngineer_HAADS.svg">**FIELD KIT** will restore health and refresh abilities. As for the latter, the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/tacticaldroid/TacticalDroid_HAADS.svg">**RESUPPLY KITS** will drop three boxes which instantly refresh abilities.

The Rebel Commando is unique for its limited supply, but the implementation was incomplete and didn't fit the nature of the game. To fix this, defeating enemies now has a chance of restoring one use of the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_StimPacks.svg">**STIM PACKS** ability, with the amount available indicated by the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_StimMeter.svg">**STIMS** meter below the rest of the abilities. Similarly, the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_Barrage.svg">**MICROGRENADE LAUNCHER** has a chance of being fully resupplied for each defeated enemy and can be unequipped and re-equipped at any time, so long as ammo is available. Replacing the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_Repeater.svg">**REPEATER** ability is the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_Flare.svg">**RESUPPLY FLARE**, which refreshes abilities and speeds up recharge times for nearby allies, along with instantly refilling the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_StimPacks.svg">**STIM PACKS** and <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/rebelcommando/RebelCommando_Barrage.svg">**MICROGRENADE LAUNCHER** abilities. As well, the Relby V-10 has been swapped out for the T-39P, establishing them as a dominant force in close quarters with a rapid-fire blaster.

For the Death Trooper, the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/deathtrooper/DeathTrooper_MotionScan.svg">**ADVANCED SENSORS** ability has been replaced with <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/deathtrooper/DeathTrooper_FiringSquad.svg">**FIRING SQUAD**, an aura which grants the user and nearby allies increased headshot damage. The <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/deathtrooper/DeathTrooper_MarksmanRush.svg">**OPERATIVE** ability, formerly called <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/deathtrooper/DeathTrooper_MarksmanRush_Old.svg">**MARKSMAN RUSH**, has been remade to buff its DLT-19D, jam nearby enemy radars, and will no longer apply a sprint speed boost.

The Royal Guards are finally in possession of their special melee weapons, with the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/royalguard/RoyalGuard_ForcePike.svg">**SINISTER STRIKES** ability replacing <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/royalguard/RoyalGuard_BurstMode.svg">**BURST MODE**. Additionally, the primary blaster has been swapped for the S-195, a semi-automatic double-barreled pistol with a high rate of fire.

The Resistance Rocket-Jumper has had its abilities remade around her already existing blaster, the **A180 RIFLE**. The <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/resistancerocketjumper/ResistanceRocketJumper_Sniper.svg">**SNIPER MODE** takes the place of the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/resistancerocketjumper/ResistanceRocketJumper_CyclerRifle.svg">**CYCLER RIFLE**, largely functioning the same but with the added bonus of instantly refreshing the ability if it is used to defeat an enemy with a headshot. As for the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/resistancerocketjumper/ResistanceRocketJumper_ConcussionDart.svg">**CONCUSSION DART**, it has been replaced by the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/resistancerocketjumper/ResistanceRocketJumper_Launcher.svg">**ION LAUNCHER MODE**, a charge-up explosive weapon effective against shields, vehicles, and groups of enemies.

The First Order Flametrooper's <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/flametrooper/Flametrooper_SplitterFire.svg">**INCENDIARY SPLITTER** has been replaced with <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/flametrooper/Flametrooper_Regen.svg">**INFERNAL RESTORATION**, which grants rapid health regeneration for a duration dependent on how many enemies were defeated prior to activation.

The Guavian Security has had its <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/guaviansecurity/GuavianSecurity_Supply.svg"> **RESERVOIR** system has been remade from the ground up to be a shared resource meter, allowing for multiple abilities to be simultaneously active at the cost of faster drain rates, and as well they can be activated at any time as opposed to requiring the meter to be fully refilled. The <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/reinfocements/guaviansecurity/GuavianSecurity_Supply.svg"> **RESERVOIR** will replenish on its own a moment after all abilities have become inactive and will forcefully deactivate them if it's fully depleted.

<h3 id="updated-heroes"><strong><img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/community-updates/Hero_Icon_01.svg"> UPDATED HEROES</strong></h3> 

![mandobanner](../assets/community-updates/v10/mando_banner.webp){ .round-corners }

As we've done with our older Reinforcements, we've also taken to revisiting some of the existing heroes.

The Mandalorian, Din Djarin, receives a new <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/dindjarin/Mando_ThermalVision.svg"> **GRAPPLE STRIKE** ability, replacing his old <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/dindjarin/Mando_ThermalVision_Old.svg"> **THERMAL VISION** ability. When activated, he fires his grappling hook at an enemy, rapidly pulling himself to them with an attack that knocks them to the ground. To go with this, his **EVASION** Star Card has been replaced with **EXTENSION CORD**, which will extend the targeting range of his <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/dindjarin/Mando_ThermalVision.svg"> **GRAPPLE STRIKE**.

Jango Fett also receives some updates to his kit, with <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/jangofett/Jango_Collecting.svg"> **ADAPTIVE ARMOR** replacing the <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/jangofett/Jango_Collecting_old.svg"> **COLLECTING BOUNTIES** ability. The intention is to reward taking higher risks, as this new ability will boost Jango's health based on the amount of enemies that were nearby upon its activation; larger groups grant a higher bonus, up to a maximum. To maintain relevance, the **JUST A SIMPLE MAN** Star Card has also been adjusted to increase the detection radius of <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/jangofett/Jango_Collecting.svg"> **ADAPTIVE ARMOR**.

Captain Rex's <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/abilities/heroes/captainrex/Rex_Fortify.svg"> **GENERATION ONE** has been revamped to grant him damage reduction, with the effect strengthening at lower health values.

Cal Kestis is now accompanied by everyone's favorite buddy droid, BD-1, who will be perched atop his back just as would be expected.

To go with his new SITH LORD appearance, Palpatine has been added to the hero roster for the Separatist Alliance, making him available to the Dark Side in all *Age of the Republic* maps.

In the interest of keeping the eras distinct from each other, Gideon Hask has been restricted to the *Age of Resistance*, with a new hero taking his place in the *Age of Rebellion* as you'll soon see. To that end, Hask's INFERNO SQUAD appearance has been removed as well.

We've also taken the time to fix all of the broken abilities and Star Cards from the base game, such as incorrect positions whenever using Yoda's <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/community-updates/v10/Yoda_Ability_02.svg"> **PRESENCE**, Bossk's <img class="h3-icon" class="no-lb" src="../../assets/community-updates/v10/Bosk_Ability_01.svg"> **SPREADING THE DISEASE** Star Card doing nothing, and much more. Many of these former issues may be recognizable from [this infamous Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/StarWarsBattlefront/comments/gcyh0r/these_are_all_the_broken_hero_star_cards/?rdt=43574).
